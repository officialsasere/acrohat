# Code of Conduct

We want Kwiiva to be a friendly place for everyone who wants to help, whatever their experience, background or country.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and say thank you.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; share other people's private information; or post spam or off-topic promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository (Security → Report a vulnerability) or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are treated confidentially.

This is a short version in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies in full.
