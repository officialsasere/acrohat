Edit text in place

Click a paragraph and type. Lines re-flow inside the original box; missing fonts are replaced with similar ones.

Edit images

Insert, move, resize, replace and delete images on the page.

Annotate & comment

Highlight, underline, strike through, draw, shapes, sticky notes and free text, saved as standard PDF annotations.

Fill, create & sign forms

Fill text fields, checkboxes, radios and dropdowns, or build your own form fields. Draw or type a signature.

Digital signatures

Sign with your own certificate (.p12 / .pfx), and see whether signed files you open are intact or were changed after signing.

Real redaction

Remove text and images from the file itself, including by pattern: emails, phone numbers, ID and card numbers.

Organize pages

Drag to reorder, rotate, delete, insert blanks, merge PDFs, extract, split, crop.

Headers, footers & watermarks

Page numbers, Bates numbering, dates, DRAFT/CONFIDENTIAL watermarks on any pages.

Scans become text (OCR)

Make scanned pages searchable, selectable and editable. Runs on your computer.

Protect & clean

AES-256 passwords with permissions, file-size reduction, remove metadata, scripts and attachments.

PDF/A & accessibility

Convert to PDF/A-2b for archiving, run a preflight check, and tag documents for screen readers with alt text.

Convert & compare

Export to DOCX, text or images; turn images into PDF; compare two versions of a document.

Private by design

No accounts, no uploads, and no tracking of your files. Fonts, OCR and the PDF engine ship inside the app. Only an anonymous launch count is kept, and you can turn it off.

Installing an unsigned app

Kwiiva Paper is a free project and the installers are not yet signed with paid developer certificates, so your OS will warn you the first time:

macOSDrag Kwiiva Paper to Applications, then right-click → Open → Open (once). If macOS says the app is "damaged", run xattr -cr "/Applications/Kwiiva Paper.app" in Terminal.
WindowsIf SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway.
LinuxMake the AppImage executable (chmod +x) and run it, or install the .deb.

Prefer to verify? The source is public and the installers are built by public GitHub Actions runs.
