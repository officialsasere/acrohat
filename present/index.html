Themes & layouts

Ten themes with matching layouts. Change the theme and every slide is recoloured; edit the slide master once and it shows everywhere.

Text that behaves

Bullets, numbering, levels, columns, line spacing and shrink-to-fit. Every font in your files, with look-alike fonts built in.

90 shapes & connectors

Arrows, callouts, flowchart symbols, stars and more, with fills, gradients, outlines, shadows and a handle to adjust each one.

Tables & charts

Tables with merged cells and banded rows. Column, bar, line, area, pie, doughnut, scatter and radar charts you edit like a small sheet.

Pictures, audio & video

Drop in pictures, crop them to a shape, add a border or shadow. Embed a clip or a sound that plays in the show.

Animations & transitions

Entrance, emphasis and exit effects in the order you choose, paragraph by paragraph, plus slide transitions with timing.

Present with confidence

Full screen, pen, highlighter and laser pointer, jump to any slide, and a presenter view with notes, next slide and a timer.

Open and save PPTX

Open a .pptx and carry on, or save one to share. Export to PDF, pictures, a single web page or a plain outline.

From an outline

Type or paste an outline and get slides in a theme in one step, or edit all your slides as text.

Never lose work

Presentations are kept on your device as you work. Undo and redo everything, however many steps back.

Phone and tablet too

Private by design

No account, no uploads, no tracking of your slides. Fonts and everything else ship inside the app.

Installing an unsigned app

Kwiiva Present is a free project and the installers are not yet signed with paid developer certificates, so your OS will warn you the first time:

macOSDrag Kwiiva Present to Applications, then right-click → Open → Open (once). If macOS says the app is "damaged", run xattr -cr "/Applications/Kwiiva Present.app" in Terminal.
WindowsIf SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway.
LinuxMake the AppImage executable (chmod +x) and run it, or install the .deb.

Prefer to verify? The source is public and the installers are built by public GitHub Actions runs.
